Our Story

We just wanted something to do.

There were gyms. There were cafes. There were malls. But when it came to actually doing something together?

Nothing.
No run club. No community. No place to show up, meet new people and just... vibe.

So we thought — why wait for someone else to start one?

Run. Meet people. Have a good time.

You don't need to be a runner. You don't need to be fast. You definitely don't need to own those ₹10,000 running shoes.

Just show up.

Because somewhere between the first kilometre, the random conversations, the terrible jokes, the post-run chai and the people you met for the first time...

Running became the excuse.
The people became the reason.

A place to show up.
  • A place where you can come alone and leave knowing people.
  • A place where your pace doesn't matter.
  • A place where nobody cares if you ran 2K or 10K.
  • A place where you don't have to be the fittest person in the group.

You just have to show up.

We didn't start a run club because everyone here was a runner. We started because nobody was doing anything like this here. So we decided to build it ourselves. One run at a time. One person at a time. One Saturday at a time.
