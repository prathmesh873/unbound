How It Works

Three steps to legendary.

No pressure. No pace requirement. Just show up, run, and collect your stamps.

01
🛂

Get Your Passport

Sign up above to create your digital Unbound Passport. You'll get a unique passport number and start with zero stamps — the blank page of your running story.

02
🏃

Show Up & Run

Join any UNBOUND Sunday run. At the finish line, scan the QR code displayed on-site. Takes 10 seconds — boom, your run is logged.

🏆

Your passport stamps up automatically. Hit 5, 10, 25, 50, or 100 runs — each milestone unlocks an exclusive physical reward. DM us to claim.

Run Verification

How we verify your runs

No apps. No GPS uploads. Just scan the QR at the finish and you're done.

📲
  • 01 Complete the run — any pace, any distance you managed.
  • 02 Find the QR code displayed at the finish line / with the UNBOUND crew.
  • 03 Scan it with your phone camera — no app needed. Fill your email.
  • 04 Our team verifies attendance and updates your passport within 24 hrs.
  • 05 Log in here to see your new stamp and updated run count. 🎉
